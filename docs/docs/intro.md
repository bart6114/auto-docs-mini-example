# Welcome to Click-a-Duck

Click-a-Duck is a quick score chase across a busy pond. Three birds are already moving when the game opens. Click one to score and send a replacement into the flock.

Most birds on the pond are ducks, worth one point. Watch for the occasional swan, worth five.

The pond grows on its own while you play. Higher scores allow more birds on screen, and new arrivals come faster until the flock reaches its limit. There is no finish line, so keep clicking for as long as you can keep up.

## At a glance

- Click a duck for one point or a swan for five. The clicked bird leaves and another takes its place.
- Climb the ranks at 5, 15, 30, and 50 points.
- Use Pause to freeze the pond and stop new arrivals.
- Use Reset to return to zero points and three birds.

Read [How to play](./product/click-a-duck.md) for the score, rank, and flock rules.
