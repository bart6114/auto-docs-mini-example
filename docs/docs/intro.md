# Welcome to Click-a-Duck

Click-a-Duck is a quick score chase across a busy pond. Three ducks are already moving when the game opens. Click one to score a point and send a replacement into the flock.

The pond grows on its own while you play. Higher scores allow more ducks on screen, and new ducks arrive faster until the flock reaches its limit. There is no finish line, so keep clicking for as long as you can keep up.

## At a glance

- Click a duck to add one point. The clicked duck leaves and another takes its place.
- Climb the ranks at 5, 15, 30, and 50 points.
- Use Pause to freeze the ducks and stop new arrivals.
- Use Reset to return to zero points and three ducks.

Read [How to play](./product/click-a-duck.md) for the score, rank, and flock rules.
